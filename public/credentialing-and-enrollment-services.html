Provider credentialing and insurance enrollment

A provider cannot bill an insurer until the enrollment is approved. We prepare, submit and track applications so new and existing providers are ready to be paid.

Request a consultationHow we work

What credentialing and enrollment mean

Credentialing is the process insurers use to verify a provider’s licence, training and history. Enrollment is the application to join a payer’s network, including Medicare and Medicaid programs. Delays at this stage mean delayed payment, so the work needs careful tracking.

What we help with

  • Provider enrollment with commercial insurers.
  • Medicare and Medicaid enrollment applications.
  • CAQH profile set-up and upkeep.
  • Re-credentialing and revalidation reminders, so renewals are not missed.
  • Follow-up with payers until each application is approved or a clear answer is recorded.

How the work runs

  1. We collect the provider documents and confirm which payers are needed.
  2. We complete and submit the applications.
  3. We follow up regularly and record every response.
  4. You receive a simple tracker showing each payer, the date submitted and the current status.

Approval times differ by payer, and they can take weeks or months. We cannot control a payer’s timeline, but we can keep the file complete and the follow-up steady.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between credentialing and enrollment?

Credentialing verifies the provider’s qualifications. Enrollment is the application to be part of an insurer’s network so the provider can bill and be paid.

How long does insurance enrollment take?

It varies by payer and can take weeks or months. Complete documents and regular follow-up help avoid avoidable delays.

Do you help with Medicare and Medicaid enrollment?

Yes, we support Medicare and Medicaid enrollment applications.

What is CAQH?

CAQH is a system many commercial insurers use to collect and verify provider information. Keeping the profile current is part of staying enrolled.
