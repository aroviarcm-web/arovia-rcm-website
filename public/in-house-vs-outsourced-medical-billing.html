In-house vs outsourced medical billing: how to decide

Neither choice is always right. It depends on your size, your staff and where your money is leaking.

Request a consultationHow we work

By Arovia RCM · Published 2026-10-25

When in-house works well

  • You have a trained biller who knows your payers and your specialty.
  • Your claims go out quickly and your aging report stays healthy.
  • You want direct control over every step.

When outsourcing helps

  • Your front desk is doing billing on top of other work.
  • Old claims keep building up because nobody has time to follow up.
  • Your biller left and recruiting is slow.
  • You want extra capacity without hiring.

A middle path

You do not have to outsource everything. Many practices keep claim entry in-house and hand over only the work that gets neglected, such as AR follow-up or denials.

Questions to ask yourself

  • How old is our oldest unpaid claim, and who is working on it?
  • How many denials did we appeal last month?
  • What does billing really cost us in staff time?
