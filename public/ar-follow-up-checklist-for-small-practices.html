An AR follow-up checklist for small practices

Accounts receivable work is easier when it follows a routine. Use this checklist as a starting point.

Request a consultationHow we work

By Arovia RCM · Published 2026-10-25

Every day

  • Review claims rejected by the clearinghouse and fix them the same day.
  • Post new payments and note any that were lower than expected.
  • Work the oldest and highest-value unpaid claims first.
  • Record every payer call: date, name, answer and next step.

Every week

  • Review the aging report and look at claims that moved into an older bucket.
  • Follow up on claims with no payer response after the normal processing time.
  • Check denials, correct what can be corrected and note the appeal deadline for the rest.
  • Send patient statements and follow up on patient balances.
  • Write down the four numbers: days in AR, clean claim rate, denial rate and collection rate.

Every month

  • Group denials by reason and payer, and pick the top three to fix at the source.
  • Compare payments with contracted rates to catch underpayments.
  • Review old balances and decide which are worth more effort and which should be adjusted.
  • Check that providers' enrollment and licences are current.

When it gets too much

If your team cannot keep up, an outside team can take over follow-up while you keep control of decisions. Read about our AR follow-up service, or use our guide to the aging report to see where your money is stuck.
