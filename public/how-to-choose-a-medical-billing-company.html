How to choose a medical billing company: 10 questions to ask

A good partner answers these clearly. Be cautious if answers are vague.

Request a consultationHow we work

By Arovia RCM · Published 2026-10-25

The questions

  1. Exactly which tasks are included and which are extra?
  2. Will you sign a Business Associate Agreement before seeing any patient data?
  3. How do you protect PHI, and who will have access?
  4. Which billing systems and clearinghouses can you work in?
  5. What will I see in reports, and how often?
  6. Who is my day-to-day contact?
  7. How do you handle denials and appeal deadlines?
  8. How are fees calculated, and is anything charged beyond that?
  9. What are the contract length and exit terms, and do I keep my data?
  10. Can I speak to a current client?

Red flags

  • A guaranteed collection result.
  • Pressure to sign quickly or a long lock-in with no exit.
  • No clear answer on who owns your data and payer accounts.
  • Reluctance to put the scope in writing.
