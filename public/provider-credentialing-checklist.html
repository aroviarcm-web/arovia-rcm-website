A provider credentialing checklist for new hires

Credentialing takes time, and a provider who is not enrolled cannot be paid. Start early and keep records in one place.

Request a consultationHow we work

By Arovia RCM · Published 2026-10-25

Start early

Payer timelines vary, and can be long. Begin as soon as a provider accepts the offer, not on the first day.

Documents to gather

  • State license and DEA registration, if applicable.
  • NPI number and a CAQH profile that is complete and current.
  • Education, training and board certification details.
  • Work history with no unexplained gaps.
  • Malpractice insurance certificate.
  • Practice details: tax ID, address and group NPI.

Track every application

Keep a simple sheet: payer, date submitted, who you spoke to, status and next follow-up date. Applications often stall without a nudge.

Do not bill before you are enrolled

Confirm the effective date with each payer before scheduling their patients. Our credentialing and enrollment service can run this process for you.
