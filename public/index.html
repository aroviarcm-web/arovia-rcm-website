Questions

Questions practice owners ask before they hire an RCM partner.

What is revenue cycle management (RCM)?

Revenue cycle management is the process of getting paid for care: checking coverage, getting approvals, coding, sending claims, posting payments, following up on unpaid claims, handling denials and billing patients.

What does an AR follow-up company do?

It tracks claims that were sent to insurers but not paid, finds out why, fixes or appeals them and reports on progress. Our team calls payers, checks portals and records every answer.

Is outsourcing medical billing worth it for a small practice?

It can be, when billing work keeps getting delayed or one person is overloaded. A baseline audit shows how your claims perform today, so you can decide with facts.

Do you guarantee collections?

No. We never promise a specific collection amount. We promise the work, the tracking and a clear weekly report.

How do we get started?

Book a call. We then run a baseline audit, agree a written scope and start a 30-day pilot that you review before deciding to continue.

Is our patient information safe with you?

A signed Business Associate Agreement, NDA and service agreement come before any patient information is shared. Our team uses company systems only, with role-based access and HIPAA training.
